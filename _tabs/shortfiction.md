What would happen if someone fell in love with a dream?

What if an alien page tried to help his master win a war that had been raging for generations?

What might it look like if extraterrestrials learned everything they knew about humanity from our radio broadcasts?

These are just a few of the strange questions I've found myself asking. Rather than doing something sensible with my time, I decided to write stories about them.

Some are funny. Some are serious. Some are a little bizarre. Some are only a few pages long, while others take their time getting wherever they're going.

They're all collected here in **Short Fiction for Short People**.

Don't worry. Tall (and regular sized people) are welcome to read it, too.
